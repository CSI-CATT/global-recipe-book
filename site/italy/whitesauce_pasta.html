Italian White Sauce Pasta

Italian White Sauce Pasta, also known as Fettuccine Alfredo, is a classic and elegant dish featuring pasta coated in a rich, creamy sauce made from butter, Parmesan cheese, and heavy cream. Beloved for its comforting and indulgent flavor, this dish is a timeless favorite in Italian-American cuisine.

Below is a simple yet luxurious recipe — smooth, creamy, and perfectly rich, made with just a few simple ingredients for an authentic Italian experience.

Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Serves: 4 people

🍞Ingredients

For the Pasta

  • 400g Fettuccine pasta
  • Salt for boiling water

For the White Sauce (Alfredo Sauce)

  • ½ cup (115g) unsalted butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1½ cups (360 ml) heavy cream
  • 1½ cups freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)
  • A pinch of nutmeg (optional)

📝Instructions

1. Cook the Pasta
  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil.
  2. Add the fettuccine and cook until al dente (firm to the bite).
  3. Before draining, reserve about 1 cup of pasta water. Drain the pasta.
2. Prepare the White Sauce
  1. In a large skillet, melt butter over medium heat.
  2. Add minced garlic and sauté for about 1 minute, until fragrant (don’t let it brown).
  3. Slowly pour in heavy cream, whisking gently. Simmer for 3–4 minutes until slightly thickened.
  4. Reduce heat to low and gradually whisk in grated Parmesan until smooth and creamy.
  5. Season with salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg (optional).
3. Combine and Serve
  1. Add the drained pasta to the skillet and toss well to coat in the sauce.
  2. If the sauce is too thick, add a splash of the reserved pasta water to adjust consistency.
  3. Garnish with fresh parsley and extra Parmesan. Serve immediately.

🍴Serving Tip

Serve hot, paired with grilled chicken or shrimp for added protein. It also goes perfectly with steamed vegetables, a fresh green salad, or crusty garlic bread. A glass of crisp white wine like Pinot Grigio complements the dish beautifully.
