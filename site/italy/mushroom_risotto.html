Mushroom Risotto

Mushroom Risotto (Risotto ai Funghi) is a classic Northern Italian dish celebrated for its creamy, velvety texture achieved through the slow cooking of Arborio rice. Each grain absorbs flavorful broth while releasing its natural starches, resulting in a rich and deeply satisfying dish without the need for heavy cream.

This recipe will guide you in preparing an authentic, comforting risotto with earthy mushrooms and a luxurious, restaurant-quality finish.

Prep Time: 20 minutes
Cook Time: 35 minutes
Serves: 4 people

🍞Ingredients

  • 6 cups (approx. 1.5 liters) chicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 400g mushrooms, sliced (cremini or a mix)
  • 1 shallot (or ½ onion), finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1½ cups (300g) Arborio rice
  • ½ cup (120 ml) dry white wine (like Pinot Grigio or Sauvignon Blanc)
  • 1 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

📝Instructions

1. Prepare the Broth and Mushrooms
  1. In a saucepan, bring broth to a simmer and keep warm over low heat.
  2. In a large skillet, heat 1 tbsp olive oil. Add mushrooms and cook 5–7 minutes until browned.
  3. Season with salt and pepper, then remove and set aside.
2. Start the Risotto
  1. In the same skillet, add remaining olive oil and butter. Sauté shallot until soft (3–4 minutes).
  2. Add minced garlic; cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Stir in Arborio rice and toast for 2 minutes until edges look translucent.
3. Cook the Risotto
  1. Pour in white wine to deglaze the pan and simmer until mostly absorbed.
  2. Add a ladle of warm broth and stir continuously until absorbed.
  3. Repeat, adding broth one ladle at a time and stirring often for about 15–20 minutes.
  4. The rice should be creamy and al dente when done.
4. Finish and Serve
  1. Stir in the cooked mushrooms, Parmesan, and parsley.
  2. Adjust seasoning with salt and pepper.
  3. Serve immediately while creamy and warm.

🍴Serving Tip

Serve hot in warm bowls and garnish with extra Parmesan, olive oil drizzle, or fresh parsley. Enjoy it with a glass of the same white wine used in cooking — it pairs beautifully with the earthy mushroom flavor.
