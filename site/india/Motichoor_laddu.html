Motichoor Laddu

A festive Indian sweet made of golden boondi pearls bound in syrupy perfection. A melt-in-mouth delight of sweetness and joy.

Ingredients

    🟠 For the Boondi:
  • Besan (gram flour) – 1 cup
  • Water – around ¾ cup (adjust to make a smooth, flowing batter)
  • Orange food color – a pinch (optional, for traditional color)
  • Ghee or oil – for deep frying
    🍯 For the Sugar Syrup:
  • Sugar – ¾ cup
  • Water – ½ cup
  • Cardamom powder – ¼ teaspoon
  • A few saffron strands (optional, for aroma and color)
  • Rose water or kewra essence – few drops (optional)
    🌰 For Garnish:
  • Chopped pistachios or almonds – 1–2 tablespoons
  • Edible silver foil (vark) – optional, for presentation
Prep: 15 min | Cook: 25 min | Set: 10 min

Preparation Method

  1. In a mixing bowl, combine besan, a pinch of orange food color, and water to make a smooth, lump-free batter of flowing consistency.
  2. Heat ghee or oil in a deep pan. Hold a perforated ladle (boondi jhara) over the pan and pour batter through it, letting small droplets fall into the hot oil.
  3. Fry the boondis on medium heat until just cooked and soft, not crispy. Drain and set aside on paper towels.
  4. In another pan, combine sugar and water to make a one-string consistency syrup. Add cardamom powder, saffron strands, and a few drops of rose water.
  5. Add the fried boondi to the warm syrup. Mix gently until well coated. Let it rest for 5–10 minutes to absorb the syrup.
  6. When the mixture is slightly warm, add chopped nuts if desired and shape into round laddus with greased hands.
  7. Garnish with pistachios or silver vark and allow to set for 10 minutes before serving.
